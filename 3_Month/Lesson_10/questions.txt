1. What is RLHF, and what are its main stages?
   --> RLHF - modelni inson fikri asosida yaxshilash usuli

   Asosiy bosqichlari:
   1. SFT - modelga tugri javob berishni urgatish
   2. Reward Model - javoblarni baholashni urgatish
   3. PPO - modelni yaxshi javob berishga u  rgatish

2. What do Chosen and Rejected responses mean?
   --> Chosen - yaxshi deb tanlangan javob
   --> Rejected - yomonroq deb tanlangan javob

3. What is the role of a Reward Model?
   --> Reward Model javoblarni baholaydi va har bir javobga ball beradi. Yaxshi javobga yuqori, yomon javobga past ball beradi

4. Explain the Bradley–Terry loss. Why does only the reward difference matter?
--> Bradley–Terry Loss - Chosen javobning Rejected javobdan yaxshiroq bulish ehtimolini hisoblaydi
     Faqat reward farqi muhim, chunki bu farq qaysi javob yaxshiroq ekanini kursatadi

5. Why does PPO use a clipped objective?
---> Clipped Objective model ni bir qadamda juda katta uzgarib ketmasligi uchun ishlatiladi

6. What are the Reference Model and the KL penalty for?
--> Reference Model - yangi modelni oldingi model bilan solishtirish uchun ishlatiladi
     KL Penalty - yangi modelning oldingi modeldan juda uzoqlashib ketmasligini taminlaydi

7. Which models are in GPU memory during PPO-based RLHF, compared with DPO?
---> PPO - Policy Model, Reference Model, Reward Model va Value Model
      DPO - Policy Model va Reference Model
      Shuning uchun DPO kamroq GPU xotirasi talab qiladi

8. What is reward hacking? Give an example and two mitigations.
---> Reward Hacking - model yaxshi ball olish uchun notugri yul tutishi
      Ex: model foydali javob berishti urniga, uzun javob yozib yuqori ball olishga harakat qiladi
      Oldini olish:
      - Reward Modelni sifatli malumotlar bilan uqitish
      - Model javoblarini inson tomonidan tekshirish

9. When would you choose DPO over PPO-based RLHF, and vice versa?
---> DPO - oddiyroq uqitish va kamroq GPU xotirasi kerak bulganda
      PPO - murakkabroq uqitish va reward orqali modelni boshqarish kerak bulganda

10. How is DPO mathematically related to KL-regularized RLHF?
---> DPO - KL bilan cheklangan RLHF asosida yasalgan usul. U alohida Reward Model va PPO ishlatmasdan, Chosen va Rejected javoblar orqali modelni tugridan-tugri uqitadi
